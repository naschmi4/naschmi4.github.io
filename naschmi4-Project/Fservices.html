Enhancements

DiamondGlow

DiamondGlow is a 6 in 1 treatment. It exfoliates, infuses, and assists in lymphangitic drainage. It also can be a lip and eye treatment. This treatment will clarify, hydrate, brighten or assist in antiaging based on the serum that is chosen. Serum options: Vitamin C, ultra-hydrating, clarifying or ultra-brightening, Tissue Nutrient Solution or TNS.

Microdermabrasion

Microdermabrasion is a non-invasive mechanical exfoliation meaning a machine is used with a diamond tip and will help remove the dead skin cells on the surface level of the face to improve skin radiance.

Pro Pen Nanoinfusion

Unlike Microneedling, Nanoneedling is noninvasive. This treatment does not wound the skin or draw blood, This treatment uses microscopic pins to infuse serums into the sking and improve the appearance of skin texture as well as improve the skin’s ability to absorb nutrients.

Pro Pen Luminfusion

LuminFusion supercharges skin smoothness and luminosity with no downtime. High-tech modalities including NanoInfusion and LED red light combined with a targeted Retinol Peel, infuse actives to reduce the appearance of skin aging and restore skin vibrancy.
