Massage Services

Soothe your body and mind with personalized massage therapy designed to release tension and renew from within.

Massages

Classic Massage

Relax with a full-body massage tailored to your ideal pressure: light, medium, or firm. Smooth, flowing strokes melt away stress and tension, leaving you relaxed and refreshed.

Hot Stone Massage

Smooth, heated stones glide across your body, melting away tension with every touch. This warming treatment soothes sore muscles, boosts circulation, and leaves you deeply relaxed.

Himalayan Salt Stone Massage

Detoxify and deeply relax. The grounding pressure of warm Himalayan salt stones calms your mind while infusing your skin with essential minerals, leaving you refreshed and renewed.

Deep Tissue Massage

Massage technique that will allow the therapist to work deeper into the tissues and to get closer to the root of your body’s issues.

Reverse compression technique that works to improve circulation and relieve muscle discomfort. Our signature glide technique helps to avoid marks on your skin.

Prenatal Massage

Find comfort and well-being in your second and third trimesters. Expert therapists use special techniques and positioning support to alleviate your stress and bring you calm.

Oncology Massage

Relax deeply in a safe, caring environment. Modified techniques and light, nurturing pressure create space for rest, comfort, and calm.
